Examination details

All calculations run on this device. No data are transmitted or automatically stored. Download a report before closing or reloading the page. Reference links require internet access.

Original MaRIA

1.5 × thickness (mm) + 0.02 × RCE + 5 × edema + 10 × ulcers

Edema and ulcers: absent = 0; present = 1. Enter noise-corrected relative contrast enhancement (RCE) as a percentage number, e.g. 100 for 100%.

Requires pre- and post-gadolinium measurements. If RCE is unavailable, leave it blank: simplified MaRIA can still be calculated.

Simplified MaRIA (sMaRIA)

1 × (thickness > 3 mm) + 1 × edema + 1 × fat stranding + 2 × ulcers

Each finding is absent = 0 or present = 1. Range: 0–5 per segment. Thickness exactly 3 mm contributes 0 points.

Does not require RCE or gadolinium-enhanced sequences for score calculation. MRI protocol selection remains a radiological decision.

Segment assessment

Select “Assessed” for each segment to include. Enter actual wall thickness and explicitly select present or absent for each finding. Missing values are never treated as zero.

This form uses six segments: terminal ileum, ascending, transverse, descending and sigmoid colon, and rectum. Other studies use different segment groupings; compare totals only when the same scheme and coverage are used. Proximal small-bowel and postoperative anatomy findings should be documented separately.

Severe-activity cutoffs were validated against endoscopic ulceration. sMaRIA ≥ 2 may result from multiple non-ulcer findings; it does not establish that an ulcer is visible on MRI. Below-cutoff scores do not exclude all Crohn’s disease activity.

For trained clinical use with MRI review. These scores measure inflammatory activity and do not quantify fibrosis or capture every complication. Interpret with symptoms, biomarkers and endoscopy; they do not independently determine treatment.

Measurement notes & references

RCE: [(WSI post − WSI pre) / WSI pre] × 100 × (SD noise pre / SD noise post). WSI = bowel-wall signal intensity; SD noise = standard deviation of background noise. Use appropriately matched acquisitions and regions of interest. This form accepts the calculated RCE; it does not derive it from MRI images.

Edema: increased mural T2 signal. Fat stranding: inflammatory change in adjacent fat. Ulcers: mural surface defects. Measure wall thickness in an adequately distended segment. sMaRIA is a separate score, not simply MaRIA with the RCE term removed.
