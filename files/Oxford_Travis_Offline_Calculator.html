High risk of IV corticosteroid non-response. In the original Oxford study, a positive day-3 result was associated with a high risk of colectomy during that admission. Contemporary outcomes may differ because rescue therapies and care pathways have evolved.
Clinical use: A positive result is a prognostic warning, not a stand-alone treatment order. It should prompt timely multidisciplinary reassessment and planning for rescue therapy and colorectal surgical input in the context of the patient's overall condition.
Medical basis
Classic Oxford/Travis criteria on day 3 of IV corticosteroids: >8 stools/day, or 3–8 stools/day plus CRP >45 mg/L.
