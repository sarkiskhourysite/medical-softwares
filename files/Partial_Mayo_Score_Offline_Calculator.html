Clinical note: A commonly used definition of clinical remission in studies is a Partial Mayo Score of ≤2 with no individual subscore >1. Definitions may vary by study or clinical context, so the score should be interpreted together with clinical judgment and, when appropriate, biomarkers and endoscopy.
