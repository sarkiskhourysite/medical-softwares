Interpretation: a MUC score >6.2 is commonly used to indicate active endoscopic disease, while lower values suggest lower inflammatory activity. The score should be interpreted together with symptoms, biomarkers, endoscopy, and the full ultrasound examination.
Medical basis
Milan Ultrasound Criteria formula: MUC = 1.4 × bowel wall thickness (mm) + 2 × color Doppler signal, with CDS coded as 0 when absent and 1 when present. A threshold of >6.2 has been validated for active endoscopic disease in ulcerative colitis.

Educational use only. This calculator does not replace trained intestinal ultrasound interpretation, clinical judgment, biomarkers, or endoscopy.
