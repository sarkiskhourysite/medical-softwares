Clinical interpretation

i0–i1Absent / minimal lesions
Original ≥i2Common recurrence threshold
i3–i4Advanced lesions

i2a and i2b distinguish location, not a proven low-risk versus high-risk hierarchy. Interpret with biomarkers, symptoms, operative anatomy and treatment history. The score alone is not a treatment prescription.

Definitions vary between studies; record the version and convention used.
