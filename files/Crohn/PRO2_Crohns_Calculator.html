Both targets must be met. This is a commonly used symptom-based clinical remission definition; trial protocols may differ.

How to interpret the result

Lower weighted scores indicate fewer symptoms. The weighted score and the separate symptom targets are not interchangeable.

Original research linked weighted mean-daily PRO2 values of approximately 8, 14 and 34 to CDAI values of 150, 220 and 450, respectively. These are research correspondences, not universal severity bands. Do not compare a seven-day weighted sum with these mean-daily values.

Low PRO2 does not prove intestinal healing. Interpret alongside fecal calprotectin, CRP, endoscopy or imaging.
