03

Laboratory & weight

Use measured values and a clinician-selected standard weight.

Criteria 7–8
7. Hematocrit difference × 6

Enter 40 for 40%, not 0.40. Points = (reference − measured hematocrit) × 6.

8. Body weight deviation × 1

Points = 100 × (standard − current weight) / standard weight. Standard weight is a reference chosen for the scoring protocol, not automatically the previous weight.

Matches your PDF worksheet: hematocrit and weight contributions retain their signs, without a floor. Some trial protocols cap the weight contribution at −10; confirm the convention before using this for a trial.
