Common interpretation

0–2Endoscopic remission
3–6Mild activity
7–15Moderate activity
≥16Severe activity

Severity cutoffs vary between studies and protocols. SES-CD does not assess proximal small-bowel disease, transmural inflammation or perianal disease.
