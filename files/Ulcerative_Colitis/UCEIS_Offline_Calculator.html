Ulcerative Colitis Endoscopic Index of Severity (UCEIS)

Offline endoscopic severity calculator for ulcerative colitis

UCEIS is calculated by adding three endoscopic descriptors: vascular pattern, bleeding, and erosions/ulcers. The standard simplified score ranges from 0 to 8.

Scoring principle: score the most severely affected area seen during the examination.

1. Vascular pattern Score 0–2

2. Bleeding Score 0–3

3. Erosions and ulcers Score 0–3
