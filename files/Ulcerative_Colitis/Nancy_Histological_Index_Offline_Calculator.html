Nancy Histological Index (NHI)

Offline histological activity classifier for ulcerative colitis

Select the biopsy findings below. The Nancy Histological Index is a stepwise classification from Grade 0 to Grade 4 based on ulceration, acute inflammatory infiltrate, and chronic inflammatory infiltrate.

Important: the NHI is not additive. The final grade is determined by the most severe histological feature present.

NHI grade interpretation

Grade 0No significant histological disease activity
Grade 1Chronic inflammatory infiltrate without acute activity
Grade 2Mildly active disease
Grade 3Moderately active disease
Grade 4Severely active disease / ulceration
Histologic remission: NHI Grade 0 represents absence of significant histological disease activity. Grades 2–4 indicate active neutrophilic inflammation, while Grade 1 represents chronic inflammatory infiltrate without acute neutrophilic activity.
