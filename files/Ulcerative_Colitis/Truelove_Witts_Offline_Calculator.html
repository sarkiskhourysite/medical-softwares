Acute severe UC criteria met: ≥6 bloody stools/day plus at least one systemic toxicity criterion. This pattern generally warrants urgent hospital-based assessment and management.
How classification is applied: Severe disease is identified by the classic ASUC rule above. Mild disease is approximated using <4 stools/day with no systemic disturbance and low inflammatory activity. Presentations between mild and severe are classified as moderate. Clinical context remains essential.
Medical basis
Classic Truelove and Witts severe disease: ≥6 bloody stools/day plus at least one of pulse >90/min, temperature >37.8°C, hemoglobin <10.5 g/dL, or ESR >30 mm/h. Modern guidance may use CRP >30 mg/L as a substitute for ESR in modified criteria.
