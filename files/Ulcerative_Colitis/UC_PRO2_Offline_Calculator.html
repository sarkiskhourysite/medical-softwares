Offline patient-reported outcome calculator for ulcerative colitis

Interpretation: UC-PRO2 is a symptom measure only. STRIDE-II-style clinical remission uses rectal bleeding = 0 and stool-frequency subscore = 0, while clinical response is commonly defined as a ≥50% reduction from baseline. Trial-specific definitions may differ. UC-PRO2 does not demonstrate endoscopic or histologic healing.
Medical basis
UC-PRO2 consists of the Mayo stool-frequency and rectal-bleeding patient-reported subscores. Treat-to-target guidance commonly defines UC clinical response as a ≥50% reduction in these PRO2 components and clinical remission as rectal bleeding = 0 with stool-frequency subscore = 0.

Educational use only. This calculator does not replace clinical assessment, biomarkers, endoscopy, histology, or treatment decisions.
