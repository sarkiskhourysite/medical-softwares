Clinical note: SCCAI is a symptom-based activity index. A score of 5 or more is commonly used as a threshold suggesting active ulcerative colitis, but the score should not be used alone to make treatment decisions. Clinical assessment, biomarkers, endoscopy, and other investigations may be required.
